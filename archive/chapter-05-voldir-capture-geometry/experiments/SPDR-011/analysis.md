# Data Analysis: SPDR-011 — Layer L1 (partial economics)

**Scope of this analysis:** L1 only, per operator authorisation. L2–L5 are NOT opened. TEST and
holdout are NOT touched. Emission: `data/nautilus_runs/SPDR-011/artifact-bundle/design.parquet`
(1,390 DESIGN rows, 65 columns), Run-1 completed 2026-07-23 04:11 UTC+1 under amendment A13.

**L1 hypothesis (design §7):** a causally known HIGH daily-volatility state, after a completed
four-hour break of the prior UTC-day range, leaves positive signed residue over one fixed
four-hour episode after taker fees, discrete funding and a 2-bps execution allowance.

**Claim boundary (design §11, binding):** spread is `UNAVAILABLE_NOT_CHARGED`;
`cost_scope = PARTIAL_FEES_FUNDING_ONLY`. Reported cost understates total cost and reported net
performance is **overstated**. The terms *fully-net*, *cost-complete*, *tradable* and
*deployable* are prohibited in any statement derived from this artifact.

### 1.1 Gate artifact had to be regenerated — flag-usage defect, not a data defect

The canonical CLI invocation the pipeline prescribes returned **`blocking_pass: false`**:

```
python -m xen.estimand_validation ../data/nautilus_runs/SPDR-011 \
  --expect BTCUSDT,ETHUSDT,SOLUSDT,DOGEUSDT,XRPUSDT
→ BLOCKING_PASS: False
```

Cause: `validate_family` forwards one `expected_instruments` list to **every** cell
(`python/src/xen/estimand_validation.py:367`). SPDR-011 emits one cell per symbol (design §13),
so each single-symbol cell was scored "missing" the other four. Per-cell breakdown showed
`manifest` as the *only* failing item:

| Cell | schema | fence | manifest | catalog_attestation | reconciliation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTCUSDT | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| ETHUSDT | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| SOLUSDT | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| DOGEUSDT | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| XRPUSDT | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |

The family-level manifest check passed in the same run (`expected` = all five, `emitted` = all
five, `missing` = []). The artifact was regenerated by
`analysis_code/gate_estimand.py`, which applies the instrument expectation at the two levels
where it is meaningful — each cell must contain its own symbol, the family must contain all
five — using only canonical `validate_run` / `validate_family` calls. Result:
**all five cells `blocking_pass: true`, family manifest complete.**

This is a usability trap in a blocking gate: the prescribed command reports a false failure for
any per-symbol-cell family. Raised for the operator in §5.

### 1.3 Leak tripwire — control is non-vacuous, and the effect collapses

| Read | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Sentinel planted effect | 50.0 bps | apparatus test on a known signal |
| Sentinel post-destroy | 9.27 bps | inside pre-frozen 99% null envelope [5.72, 12.28] |
| Derangement fixed points | 0 | proper derangement (L-28 satisfied) |
| Observed raw effect | 10.78 bps | L3 conversion residue |
| Destroyed median | 0.79 bps | — |
| **Collapse fraction** | **0.074** | destroyed effect is 7.4% of raw |
| Empirical one-sided p | 0.2795 | — |

The control destroys a **planted** 50-bps effect down into its null envelope, so it is sensitive
to the mechanism it is meant to break — not a mean-invariant permutation (L-15 / EXP-012 shape).
Applied to the observed data the effect collapses to 7.4% of raw. **No surviving edge ⇒ no leak.**

The shipped `integrity_status` reads `NOT_APPLICABLE` (`applicability: RAW_L3_NOT_SUPPORTED`)
because the hard rule only engages when the raw effect is itself supported (raw ≥ 15 bps and all
five date-block CI lowers > 0); here raw = 10.78 and the CI lowers are all negative. That is the
correct behaviour of a tripwire with nothing to catch — **it is not evidence of an effect, and
these L3-flavoured numbers are reported here as integrity evidence only. The L3 value read is
NOT opened by this analysis.**

## 4. Evidence FOR the hypothesis

Reported with equal diligence; it is thin.

1. **The pooled HIGH mean is positive at every allowance level.** +11.37 / +9.37 / +6.37 bps at
   0 / 2 / 5 bps allowance (n=394). The sign survives the frozen cost map: mean gross 22.81 bps
   against 11.45 bps of charged cost leaves a positive residue.
2. **Two symbols carry large positive means.** SOLUSDT +58.80 (n=115) and DOGEUSDT +47.45
   (n=71). Both are the higher-count strata.
3. **Gross is positive before costs.** 22.81 bps [−16.71, 66.83], so the object is not killed by
   the fee schedule alone — cost sensitivity shows the mean stays positive out to a 30 bps
   round trip only turning negative at −7.19 there (CI never excludes zero anywhere).
4. **ETHUSDT median is the one positive robust stat** (+5.85 with a 0.519 win-rate) — the single
   stratum where more than half the legs pay.
5. **Occupancy matches the design story.** 0.055–0.116 of wall clock per symbol (8.0% pooled
   across the five-symbol book). This is a genuinely part-time object, as specified — not a grid
   masquerading as an event strategy.

## 5. Evidence AGAINST the hypothesis

1. **A single date supplies 99% of the pooled total.** Pooled total = 3,690 bps; **2022-10-29
   alone contributes 3,635 bps**. Removing that one date drops the pooled mean from +9.37 to
   **+0.14 bps**. (`analysis_code/l1_falsification.py` P4.)
2. **The result inverts under the design's own independence unit.** Design §3.3 states the
   independent unit is one UTC date. Event-weighted mean +9.37 → **date-weighted mean −15.31 bps**.
   The sign of the headline depends on counting 394 dependent events instead of 74 dates.
3. **Every robust central estimate is negative, in every stratum.** Pooled median −23.51,
   20% trimmed mean −27.42. Per symbol, four of five medians and four of five trimmed means are
   negative; SOL and DOGE — the two positive means — have medians of −30.58 and −26.22.
   The mean and the median disagree in sign for three of five symbols.
4. **The edge is pure tail, and the tail is thin.** Dropping the top 1 leg takes the pooled mean
   9.37 → 1.92; top 3 → −9.90; top 5 → −17.78; top 10 → −32.35. The top 5 legs contribute 287%
   of the pooled total and the top decile 891%. The body of the distribution (|x| ≤ 500 bps,
   351 of 394 legs) has mean **−26.55 bps**.
5. **Fewer than half the legs pay.** 173/394 positive = 0.439, i.e. 6.1 percentage points
   *worse* than a coin flip, on a distribution-free sign read that ignores magnitude entirely.
6. **No CI clears zero on the positive side — anywhere.** Pooled CI [−30.15, 53.39]. The only
   stratum whose CI excludes zero is **XRPUSDT, and it is negative**: −50.60 [−94.24, −18.17].
7. **Pooling is not legitimate.** Per-symbol means span 109.4 bps (−50.6 to +58.8). Design §10
   requires homogeneity before a pooled figure supports a common claim; that condition fails, so
   the pooled +9.37 is disclosure only, not a finding.
8. **Leave-one-out is unstable in both directions.** Without SOLUSDT the pooled mean is −11.01;
   without XRPUSDT it is +29.76. One symbol moves the headline by 40 bps.
9. **One time-third carries everything.** T3 (2023-01-04..03-01, only 50 events) has mean +80.45;
   the pooled mean without T3 is **−0.97**. T1 in-sample mean is −5.94.
10. **Chronological halves disagree.** First half −9.88 (median −53.09), second half +28.61
    (median −4.95). No stability across time.
11. **Signal-to-noise is negligible.** Pooled std is 400.92 bps against a 9.37 bps mean —
    a ratio of 0.023. Winsorising at q01/q99 halves the mean to +4.71.
12. **The whole run is UNPOWERED.** Pooled MDE 36.76 bps; per-symbol 39.1–95.6 bps. The design's
    SUPPORTED band needs +10 bps with CI low > 0 — an effect this emission structurally cannot
    resolve. A negative here is therefore "cannot see", not "proven absent".

## 6. Anomalies & open questions

1. **Blocking-gate usability defect (raised for action).** The pipeline's prescribed
   `python -m xen.estimand_validation <family_root> --expect <all instruments>` returns a false
   `blocking_pass: false` for any family whose cells are per-symbol, because `validate_family`
   forwards one expectation list to every cell (`src/xen/estimand_validation.py:367`). A gate
   that cries wolf trains operators to wave it through. Suggested fix: have `validate_family`
   scope each cell's expectation to that cell's own instrument and assert the full set at family
   level — which is what `analysis_code/gate_estimand.py` does. Worth a knowledge-base entry.
2. **The four unavailable events are all MID/LOW** (3 DOGE, 1 SOL; `EXIT_4H_MARK_MISSING` ×3,
   `ENTRY_MARK_MISSING` ×1). **None is HIGH**, so attrition cannot bias the L1 read. Noted and
   closed.
3. **Dependence is heavier than the event count suggests.** 394 HIGH events sit on 74 dates and
   20 weeks; 11 timestamps carry all five symbols simultaneously, 14 carry four. Any inference
   treating legs as independent overstates precision by roughly a factor of √5 at those stamps.
4. **The gate's `physicality` block is not the L1 estimand.** It reports per-cell all-tercile
   figures (e.g. BTCUSDT `total_net_bps` 3,091, Sharpe 1.77, occupancy 0.198 across 272 legs).
   Those cover HIGH+MID+LOW and are disclosure only; they must not be read as the HIGH-state
   result. Flagged because the numbers are attractive and adjacent.
5. **Not opened by this analysis:** whether HIGH beats MID/LOW (L2/L3), TOP2 selection (L4), or
   flow (L5). The L3-flavoured figures quoted in §1.3 are integrity evidence for the leak
   tripwire only.

## 7. Recommended verdict (experiment hypothesis only — NOT final, NOT family)

- **Recommendation: NOT SUPPORTED at L1, and simultaneously UNPOWERED.**

  Precisely: the emission gives **no evidence that HIGH-volatility breakouts leave positive
  signed residue after partial costs**, and it also **cannot rule out a small positive effect**.
  Both halves matter. The apparent pooled positive is a single-date artifact; but the run's
  resolution floor (MDE 36.8 bps pooled) is far above the 10-bps effect the design set out to
  detect, so this is not a clean refutation either.

- **Driven by:**
  1. One date (2022-10-29) supplies 99% of the pooled total; removing it leaves +0.14 bps.
  2. Under the design's own independence unit (UTC dates), the headline flips sign to −15.31 bps;
     every robust statistic (median, trimmed mean, body-only mean, sign test) is negative.
  3. Every stratum is UNPOWERED by the design's predeclared band, and no CI clears zero positive.

- **Would change if:** a materially larger sample — the registered CONFIRM band adds 490 HIGH
  events on 255 dates, roughly 3.4× the dates — showed a positive mean that (a) survived dropping
  the top date and top 5 legs, (b) held under date-weighting, and (c) carried a median with the
  same sign. Any one of those failing would reproduce the present picture at larger n.

- **Caveat that strengthens the negative:** spread is not charged anywhere in these figures
  (`PARTIAL_FEES_FUNDING_ONLY`). Every number above is **overstated**. A true-cost read can only
  be worse, never better. No claim of *fully-net*, *cost-complete*, *tradable* or *deployable* is
  made or supportable.

**Final verdict is the operator's.** Suggested probes if you want to push further:
- **Push on the tail:** is 2022-10-29 a real regime event or a data artifact? One date deciding
  the layer deserves a direct look at those legs before anything else.
- **Push on power:** re-derive the CONFIRM prospective MDE against the *realised* sigma (400.92
  bps, far above the 100-bps assumption in design §3.3) — the census power table may be
  optimistic by ~4×, which would make CONFIRM unpowered too and is decision-relevant before
  spending it.
- **Push on the object:** the mean/median sign conflict suggests the mechanism, if any, is a
  rare-large-move phenomenon, not a per-episode edge. That is a different object from the one
  L1 registered.
