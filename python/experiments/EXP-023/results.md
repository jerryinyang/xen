# Results: Experiment EXP-023 — AVWAP Baseline Candidate Screen

## Summary

The registered CF-AVWAP-001 baseline signal, generated by the cTrader
strategy-host branch and evaluated on emitted real OHLC prices, **did not qualify
under any component of the frozen Phase 004 suite**. All 12 instrument×domain
cTrader runs were admitted (12/12), the C# AVWAP port reproduced the `xen.avwap`
reference exactly, and every cell was reportable with an aligned same-feed
Donchian(20) reference — so the screen ran cleanly to completion. Across all 12
cells: 0 strict-gate passes, 0 ratified-loose/fallback passes, and 0 revised
portfolio-fitness passes. Standalone net effects and incremental edges sat far
below their frozen domain detection floors in every cell. This meets the scope's
predeclared **Evidence-AGAINST** criteria. **Verdict: REFUTED.**

## Detailed Findings

### 1. The screen is complete and admissible — REFUTED, not BLOCKED/INCONCLUSIVE

- **Observation**: all dependency gates passed (`dependency_manifest.csv` 34/34
  PASS), all 12 cTrader cells were admitted (`run_manifest.csv`: `admitted=true`,
  `same_feed_ok=true`, `feed_max_abs_diff=0.0`, `n_time_mismatch=0` ×12), the
  holdout fence held in every cell (`holdout_fence_checks.csv`: max
  `SourceCloseTime` < `AnalysisEndUtc`, candidate and reference), and the C#
  transcription smoke PASSED on 5m/1h/4h (`csharp_avwap_smoke_checks.csv`: 0
  field mismatch, `max_abs_price_diff=0.0`).
- **Evidence**: `run_metadata.json` `overall_status=REFUTED`, `blockers=[]`,
  `pass_tally={strict:0, loose:0, incremental:0, suite_pass:0, reportable:12}`.
- **Interpretation**: the scope's inconclusive/blocked conditions (missing
  dependency, <3 instruments/domain admitted, unaligned reference, suite cannot
  run, sparse/non-finite gating metrics, failed smoke) are all absent. With all
  12 cells reportable and no suite pass, the predeclared Evidence-AGAINST branch
  is the correct label, not a blocked or inconclusive screen.

### 2. Standalone frozen-suite screen: no strict or loose pass on any cell

- **Observation**: every standalone net effect is far below its strict MDE.
- **Evidence** (`standalone_suite_verdicts.csv`, strict MDE 1.0/4.0/12.0 bps for
  5m/1h/4h): effects range −1.41 bps (BTCUSD/4h) to +0.21 bps (EURUSD/4h, the
  only domain-4h cell with a positive point). The largest standalone effect
  (+0.21 bps) is ~57× below the 4h strict floor (12.0). Every `ci_lower_bps` is
  below the ratified-loose τ (0.375/0.375/1.5 bps) — e.g. the highest,
  EURUSD/1h, is −0.127 — so the loose L5 leg cannot pass either. Result: 0/12
  strict, 0/12 effective-loose. See `plots/effect_forest.png` and
  `plots/suite_verdict_matrix.png`.
- **Interpretation**: as a standalone always-on tradable position net of cost,
  the baseline carries no statistically positive edge at the frozen referee's
  sensitivity. This places it with the EXP-004/009 untuned-strategy lower anchor,
  not above any domain floor.

### 3. Revised portfolio-fitness vs Donchian(20): no positive-incremental pass

- **Observation**: AVWAP adds no detectable edge beyond the existing
  D-dogfood-book reference.
- **Evidence** (`portfolio_fitness_verdicts.csv`, incremental floors 12/16/32 bps;
  all 12 reportable, `n_reference_unaligned=0`, denominator 155–15,951):
  incremental edges range −11.90 bps (USTEC/4h) to +6.05 bps (XAUUSD/4h). Two 4h
  cells show small positive points — EURUSD/4h +4.39 (`ci_lower` +0.31) and
  XAUUSD/4h +6.05 (`ci_lower` −3.62) — but both are far below the 32 bps 4h
  incremental floor, and one has a CI straddling zero. Result: 0/12
  `positive_incremental`.
- **Interpretation**: the marginal AVWAP position over Donchian(20) does not
  clear the (deliberately coarse) revised-incremental detection floor in any
  cell. The small positive 4h points are below floor with near-/sub-zero lower
  CIs and must not be read as edge.

### 4. Metric book: high favorable-target rate, ~zero-to-negative net expectancy

- **Observation**: the original AVWAP strategy reaches its favorable target more
  often than not, yet does not make money net of cost.
- **Evidence** (`strategy_metric_book.csv`, real `RealClose` basis):
  `successful_bounce_rate` (favorable/(favorable+adverse)) is 0.61–0.80 across
  cells, while `model_net_bps` is ~0-to-negative everywhere (BTCUSD/5m −0.74,
  BTCUSD/4h −1.36; only EURUSD/4h marginally +0.08). `lifetime_expectancy_bps`
  is mixed and mostly negative (BTCUSD/4h −30.3; EURUSD/4h +8.0). The model
  risk-adjusted level (mean/std) is negative in nearly every cell versus a small
  positive raw mean/std (`plots/risk_adjusted_heatmap.png`,
  `plots/model_vs_raw_cumulative_returns.png`).
- **Interpretation**: the tension is mechanical, not contradictory.
  `successful_bounce_rate` counts only target-resolved moves and **excludes
  trend-change exits**, whereas `lifetime_expectancy_bps` and `model_net_bps`
  **include** trend-change exits and per-trade cost. A 60–80% favorable-target
  rate is consistent with ~0/negative expectancy when trend-change exits absorb
  larger adverse moves and cost is charged on every active bar. The
  `plots/event_trade_outcome_composition.png` shows the favorable/adverse/
  trend-change/unfinished mix that drives this.

### 5. No conflict with EXP-020/021/022

- **Observation**: positive component evidence (substrate readiness, fixed-horizon
  bounce reaction, lifetime-move study) did not carry through to a qualifying
  full strategy.
- **Interpretation**: EXP-021/022 measured **conditional event behavior** —
  direction-signed reaction at a fixed horizon and band-target/trend-change move
  completion versus look-ahead-safe benchmarks. EXP-023 instead tests a
  **tradable, always-on, cost-bearing position** through a **stringent frozen
  referee**. A conditional edge around an event can be real and still fail to
  survive as a continuously-held strategy once cost, holding periods, and
  trend-change exits are imposed and judged against a detection floor. The
  baseline is the strict first branch; it is not tuned and was never expected to
  be the strongest operationalization.

## Hypothesis Verdict

**REFUTED.**

The predeclared Evidence-AGAINST criteria are met in full: all dependency and
run-admission checks passed, all 12 instrument/domain cells are reportable
against an aligned same-feed reference, no suite component (strict,
ratified-loose/fallback, or revised portfolio-fitness) passes any cell, and both
standalone and incremental effects lie below their frozen domain detection floors
in every cell. The baseline CF-AVWAP-001 signal does not qualify under the frozen
Phase 004 suite on the first-70% analysis set.

## Limitations

- **Single untuned frozen branch.** This screens exactly one definition
  (MA(20,50) regime, typical-price AVWAP, `TickVolume**0.75`, MAD band ×1.0,
  registered lifetime exits). It does not test other regime detectors, weights,
  bands, or exit logic, and no parameter was tuned — by design.
- **Analysis set only.** All results are on the first-70% chronological slice;
  the final 30% global holdout remained sealed. No out-of-sample confirmation is
  claimed or possible here.
- **Coarse incremental floor.** The revised portfolio-fitness floors (12/16/32
  bps) are the suite's least sensitive component; a sub-floor positive marginal
  edge would be undetectable here. The small positive 4h incremental points are
  below floor with CIs at/through zero and are **not** evidence of edge.
- **Sparse-strategy risk metric.** The primary robust mean/MAD model risk level
  is undefined (MAD=0; the strategy is flat ~93% of bars), so the model-vs-raw
  risk comparison uses the finite mean/std diagnostic. This is a reporting
  fallback, not a tradability claim, and both estimators agree the model is not
  risk-superior to raw holding.

## Alternative Explanations

- **Cost, not signal, dominates.** The minimal-baseline (gross) standalone effects
  are also non-positive, so the rejection is not purely a cost artifact — but
  cost on every active bar plus trend-change exits clearly pushes a high-hit-rate
  signal to negative net expectancy.
- **Detection-floor stringency.** A genuine but small positive edge below the
  frozen floors would also produce this exact 0/12 outcome; the screen bounds the
  edge to below-floor, it does not prove exactly zero. This is the intended
  conservative reading of a frozen-suite REFUTED.
