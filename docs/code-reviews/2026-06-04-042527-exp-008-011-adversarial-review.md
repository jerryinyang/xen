## Summary

The decisive issue is **F01**: EXP-010's multi-fold confidence interval was the spread of
*fold-sized* means, not of the *pooled-OOS* mean it reported, so walk-forward draws got an
artificially wide CI (2.4× wider than single despite 67% more effective sample) that pushed
their grid MDE up one step and produced a spurious "PARTIALLY REFUTED". The fix makes the
combination a stratified bootstrap of the pooled OOS mean — bit-identical to the frozen
referee for the single fold, and correctly precision-scaled for multi-fold — which is why
the single-split reproduction anchor (F03) could never have caught the bug. EXP-011 was
internally consistent but propagated the EXP-010 conclusion as a static caveat (F06) and
soft-gated its scoped overlays (F05); both are now data-derived and hard-gated. EXP-008's
H-pool verdict is genuinely supported (the per-instrument TPR crossings are real), but its
"material" criterion only resolves to one grid step (F07) — now documented, with a faithful
within-grid fix and monotonicity reporting. Remaining items (Loss C degeneracy, Loss A
sub-material trade, EXP-009 framing, exploratory status labels) are disclosure/labelling
corrections.
