Standalone high/low volatility was weak; `DI×VOL_HI` and `DI_ADX×VOL_HI` amplified BTC/SOL
directional continuation, and BTC retained positive gross lower bounds in XENA. Costs killed
all 72 cells and no untouched OOS remains. Drift/beta/volatility clustering are controls for a
residual-alpha claim, but may be the intended exposures of a separately risk-managed product.
